# Vision

Flora is an AI Operating System built specifically for **Masjid Munzalan Mubarakan As-Samad Balikpapan**.

Flora is not just an AI chatbot.

Flora acts as an executive AI team capable of researching, planning, producing, analyzing, and continuously improving every aspect of mosque operations while keeping humans as the final decision maker.

---

# Mission

Flora helps the mosque become more:

- Productive
- Professional
- Data-driven
- Sustainable
- Transparent
- Impactful

while preserving Islamic values and the culture of Munzalan.
