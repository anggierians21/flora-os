# History of Masjid Munzalan Mubarakan As-Samad Balikpapan

> Version 1.0  
> Last Updated: August 2026

---

# Overview

Masjid Munzalan Mubarakan As-Samad Balikpapan is a branch of the Munzalan Mubarakan movement that grew from a simple dakwah movement into a center of worship, education, social empowerment, and community development in Balikpapan, East Kalimantan.

The mosque was not built merely as a physical structure. It represents years of da'wah, sacrifice, generosity, collaboration, and trust from the Muslim community.

Every wall, every program, and every activity reflects the spirit of serving Allah SWT through sincere action.

---

# Early Beginning (2018)

The story of Munzalan in Balikpapan began around 2018.

At that time there was no mosque building.

There was no large organization.

There were no permanent facilities.

What existed was only a group of young Muslims carrying the same mission:

> Bringing people closer to Allah through real action.

One of the earliest movements introduced to the community was **Gerakan Infaq Beras (GIB)**.

Rather than asking people to donate large amounts of money, the movement invited Muslims to consistently donate rice.

The collected rice was distributed to:

- Santri
- Tahfidz students
- Orphans
- Poor families
- Fi Sabilillah

This movement became the first gateway for many people to know Munzalan.

---

# Building the Community (2019–2020)

As the movement continued, more volunteers joined.

A strong culture of sincerity, discipline, and teamwork began to develop.

Young people became increasingly active in serving the community.

From this spirit emerged **PASKAS (Pasukan Amal Sholeh)**.

PASKAS became a community where Muslim youth learned leadership through service.

Members were encouraged to contribute their energy, time, and ideas for the benefit of Islam.

The focus was never personal popularity.

The focus was always serving Allah.

---

# A Historic Waqf (December 2021)

One of the greatest milestones happened in December 2021.

A generous donor, **Bapak H. Mursid**, entrusted approximately **1,588 square meters of land** as waqf through Yayasan Baitulmaal Munzalan Indonesia.

This event transformed the dream of having a permanent mosque into reality.

The waqf became the foundation for building a mosque that would serve future generations.

Without this contribution, the construction of the mosque would not have been possible.

---

# Groundbreaking Ceremony (19 June 2022)

On 19 June 2022, the official groundbreaking ceremony was held.

Community leaders, donors, volunteers, and local residents gathered together to witness the beginning of construction.

This marked the official birth of Masjid Munzalan Mubarakan As-Samad Balikpapan.

---

# Land Preparation (July 2022)

Following the groundbreaking, several technical preparations were completed:

- Land measurement
- Land boundary confirmation
- Qibla direction survey
- Administrative documentation

These preparations ensured the construction followed proper planning.

---

# Construction Begins (August 2022)

Construction officially started in August 2022.

The mosque was built gradually according to the available waqf and public donations.

Many individuals contributed in different ways:

- Financial donations
- Construction materials
- Labor
- Volunteer work
- Continuous prayers

The mosque became a symbol of collective effort.

---

# First Congregational Prayers (Ramadan 1444 H / March 2023)

Although construction was still ongoing, the mosque began serving the community during Ramadan 1444 H.

The first activities included:

- Five daily prayers
- Tarawih prayers
- Witr prayers
- Friday prayers
- Islamic studies

The building was still simple.

However, the spirit of worship was extraordinary.

---

# Growth as a Community Center (2023–2025)

After becoming operational, the mosque expanded far beyond its role as a place of prayer.

It gradually developed into a center for:

## Education

- TPQ
- Qur'an learning
- Children's education

## Dakwah

- Islamic lectures
- Weekly studies
- Community education

## Social Programs

- Food distribution
- Charity
- Zakat
- Infaq
- Waqf

## Youth Development

- PASKAS
- Volunteer programs
- Leadership development

## Mosque Operations

- Daily management
- Administration
- Maintenance
- Community engagement

---

# Legal Waqf Documentation (1 August 2025)

On 1 August 2025, the official waqf declaration (Ikrar Wakaf) was signed.

This strengthened the legal status of the mosque and ensured its protection as a waqf asset for future generations.

---

# New Identity (1 Muharram 1448 H / 2026)

A major organizational milestone occurred on the Islamic New Year.

The Munzalan movement officially adopted a new identity.

The mosque changed its official name from:

**Masjid Kapal Munzalan As-Samad**

to

**Masjid Munzalan Mubarakan As-Samad Balikpapan**

This change reflected the evolution of the movement while preserving its original values and mission.

The new identity symbolizes a stronger organizational vision and a unified direction across the Munzalan ecosystem.
